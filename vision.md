# Vision

## Product Vision

Create the operational memory of a construction company by turning everyday jobsite communication into reliable, searchable, and actionable construction reports.

## Problem

Construction updates often live in scattered WhatsApp conversations, voice notes, photos, notebooks, spreadsheets, and informal messages. This makes it hard to know what happened, who was responsible, what blocked progress, and which records can be trusted later.

## Desired Future

A person at the jobsite should be able to speak or type naturally, and the system should transform that communication into a useful report without forcing the field team into heavy forms.

Managers should have dashboards that show execution status, workforce, blockers, risks, missing reports, and historical evidence. The company should gain structure without losing the speed of WhatsApp.

## Product Principles

- Field-first: reporting must be fast for people at the jobsite.
- Human-confirmed: AI can draft reports, but uncertain information must be reviewed.
- Suggested, not assumed: external data such as weather can prefill a report, but jobsite reality must be validated by the user.
- Evidence-based: every structured report should keep links to source messages, audio, photos, and documents.
- Searchable history: past reports should be easy to find and question.
- Operational clarity: dashboards should help decisions, not just display charts.
- Integration-ready: the platform should be prepared for ERP, finance, inventory, scheduling, and client reporting.

## Success Criteria

- Field teams can send reports without changing their normal WhatsApp behavior.
- Audio messages become accurate, reviewable text.
- Managers can see daily activity and blockers from a dashboard.
- Reports can be approved, corrected, and audited.
- Users can search historical reports by project, date, activity, material, issue, or person.
- The system reduces missing or incomplete daily reports.

## Long-Term Direction

The app can grow from a construction report hub into a broader construction operations platform, connecting reporting, production planning, financial control, purchasing, inventory, document management, quality, safety, and client communication.
